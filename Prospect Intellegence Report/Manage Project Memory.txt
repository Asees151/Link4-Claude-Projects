Key learnings & principles

JavaScript-rendered sites (e.g., Square Online platforms) cannot be reliably fetched directly; web search targeting specific operational details is more productive
The most information-dense sources for agricultural prospects have been lender/credit customer features, regional tourism/chamber profiles, trade publications (Issuu), and ZoomInfo for address/tech stack confirmation
Revenue and headcount data for private ag businesses is often conflicting or unavailable and should be treated as unknown rather than asserted
File presence and byte-level consistency should be verified via ls -la before assuming prior session work is complete — compaction summaries alone are not reliable
